## Copy

Notion's second-biggest market outside the US was Japan before Notion had a Japanese product.

The community got there first. Japanese users ran their own meetups, built their own forums, and wrote and self-published their own books about a tool that only spoke English.

Notion localised in October 2021, after the demand already existed. In the year before that launch, daily active users in Japan grew 500%.

The unlock was sequencing, not translation.
