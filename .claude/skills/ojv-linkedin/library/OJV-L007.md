## Copy

Nobody wants to hear this, but a longer investor list usually means a slower raise.

Give us an hour with your list and we can usually tell you why it is not converting.

We start with four things:

→ Stage fit
→ Category history
→ Geography
→ Route in

Then we look at what is underneath them.

How many names on that list have led a round at your stage in the last 18 months? Not participated. Led.

How many have funded your category since it stopped being fashionable?

For how many do you have a warm path that is not a cold LinkedIn message?

And when you do get the meeting, is your first slide answering the question that fund actually asks, or the one you wish it asked?

Most lists fail on the first and the third. The deck is usually fine. The deck is almost always fine.

So we cut the list, we fix who is on it, and we work the warm paths into the rooms where your story already makes sense. We do not promise the yes, because nobody honest can.
