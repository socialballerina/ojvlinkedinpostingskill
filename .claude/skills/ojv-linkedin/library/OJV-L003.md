## Copy

Would you bet against Chinese biotech because you cannot name a single Chinese drug brand?

You will not find the brand, because the brand was never the plan.

Out-licensing deals from the greater China region rose nearly tenfold from 2021 to reach $137.7 billion in 2025. By September 2026 the average deal size had already hit $1.3 billion, up 76% on 2025, and the average upfront fee was $77.7 million, double the year before.

The mechanism is the part worth studying. These companies are not building American salesforces. They are selling the asset to a partner that already has one, and taking the upfront instead of the brand.

AstraZeneca's weight-loss agreement with CSPC Pharmaceutical is worth up to $18.5 billion. AbbVie's deal with RemeGen runs to $5.6 billion.

This is what founders in Asia keep missing about going west. The hard part is rarely the product. It is the buyer, the pricing and the story, and there is more than one way to solve for all three. Sometimes the answer is not to enter the market. It is to sell to whoever already has.

We had nothing to do with these deals. We just think it is the clearest example around.

What would you give up to reach the buyer two years earlier?
