## Copy

39.9%. That is one company's share of every EV battery installed on earth in the first seven months of 2026.

CATL put 289.6 GWh into vehicles over that period, up 26.6% year on year. The seven Chinese companies in the global top ten held 72.8% between them, three points more than a year earlier.

The usual read is that this is a trade problem, and that tariffs and local content rules will unwind it.

Watch what CATL is doing with the local content rules instead. It is building a plant at Debrecen in Hungary, a 7.3 billion euro investment with 100 GWh of planned annual capacity, and putting cathode and anode plants in Hungary and Morocco to feed it.

Read that again. A rule designed to keep a supply chain out is being answered by moving the supply chain in.

This is the thing a spreadsheet will not tell you. The durable asset is not the cell. It is the ability to rebuild a supply chain inside somebody else's border faster than the policy cycle can move.
