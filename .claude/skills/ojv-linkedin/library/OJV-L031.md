## Copy

354,000. That is how many industrial robots China installed in 2025, 59% of every new robot put to work worldwide.

The number is not the news. Who sold them is.

The International Federation of Robotics published the figures in September 2026. China's installations grew 20% in 2025 and beat the previous annual record by almost 60,000 units.

Chinese manufacturers supplied 195,000 of them, a 55% share of their home market. In 2024 that share was 57%.

Read from a desk in London or New York, the first number says China is automating and the second says foreign suppliers are being pushed out. Neither is quite right. Local brands now outsell foreign ones at home, and foreign brands still put in roughly 45% of a record year.

What that means for a buyer is a credible local option for most jobs, which changes every conversation about price and lead time. You only feel that on the ground, standing next to the integrator who quotes both.

A report gives you the share. It does not give you the room where the share gets decided.
