## Copy

Companies say they want pipeline in a new APAC market, then run every touch from a desk that has never been there.

Give us an hour with your outbound and we can usually tell why that market is not replying.

We start with four things:

→ Who you target
→ What you send
→ Who sends it
→ What happens after yes

Then we look at what is underneath them.

Was your list built for how that market's companies are actually organised, or copied from home with the country field changed?

Does your first message mention anything a buyer there would recognise, or could it have gone to anyone, anywhere?

Who is the sender? A name the buyer can place, a partner they already trust, or a stranger in another time zone?

When someone says yes, who takes the meeting, at what hour, and how fast?

From the inside, a silent market looks like a market that is not ready. Sometimes it is. More often the outreach was never built for it, and more volume does not fix that.

APAC is not one market. It is 15+ ecosystems with different rules, cultures and buyer behaviour.

If you are selling into an APAC market with no local team and the outreach has gone quiet, work with us.

DM us, or write to us at hello@oj.ventures
