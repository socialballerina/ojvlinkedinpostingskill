Insta360 took 47% of its 2024 revenue from Europe and the United States. Not by accident, but by design.

The cameras were already good. That is the least interesting part of the story.

What it did first was choose a buyer. 54% of its 2024 revenue came from developed countries with low price sensitivity. It sold to people who would pay for a premium device, not to people comparing on price.

Then it went to the shelf where that buyer already stands. Apple Store and Best Buy, and by its 2025 listing more than 10,000 retail stores and over 90 airports worldwide. Revenue went from 2.04 billion yuan in 2022 to 5.57 billion in 2024.

The West then pushed back in a very Western way. In February 2026 the US International Trade Commission found Insta360 had infringed a GoPro design patent. Insta360 says its existing products can still be sold in the US, and both sides have appealed. First-half 2026 revenue still grew 50.29%, while net profit fell 94.15%.

Buyer, price, shelf, and then the legal terrain an incumbent knows better than you do. None of it is product work.
