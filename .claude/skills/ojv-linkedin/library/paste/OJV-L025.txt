Would you bet against Apple in India because it arrived late? Its first stores of its own there opened in 2023.

Apple's retail presence in India began that year with Apple BKC in Mumbai and Apple Saket in Delhi. In February 2026 Apple Borivali opened as its second store in Mumbai and its sixth in India.

Between those openings, on the October 2025 earnings call, Tim Cook, then chief executive, said Apple had set an all-time revenue record in India.

The unlock was distribution it owned, sitting on a supply chain that had moved closer. India's iPhone exports reached Rs 2 trillion in the eleven months to February 2026, up from Rs 1.5 trillion in the previous financial year.

What Apple did not do was rush the shopfront. Six stores in three years is a handful of flagships, not a land grab.
