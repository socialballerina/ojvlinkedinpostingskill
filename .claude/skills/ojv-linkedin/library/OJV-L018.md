## Copy

Domino's had 1,325 stores in the UK and 1,321 in China at the end of 2025. In India it had 2,396. Here is exactly how it got there:

Those are the counts in Domino's own annual filing, as at 28 December 2025. India was its largest market outside the US by store count.

→ A local operator, not a subsidiary. India is run by Jubilant FoodWorks, a listed Indian company, as master franchisee. Domino's says prospective master franchisees must possess local market knowledge.

→ A local menu. The filing names the Chicken Burst Pizza, a double layer of chicken toppings, as an India offering.

→ A long commitment. In March 2026 the agreement was renewed for another 15 years, with an option for a further decade.

The unlock was the partner. What Domino's did not do was try to run India from head office.

That is not a one-off. Eight of its ten largest international markets were run by master franchise companies listed on stock exchanges.
