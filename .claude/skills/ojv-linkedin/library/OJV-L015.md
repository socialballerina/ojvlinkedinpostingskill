## Copy

Would you bet against a Chinese retailer in America because it owns almost none of the characters on its shelves?

MINISO's US sales grew 52.62% in 2025, by the National Retail Federation's count. It ended the year with 351 US stores, 76 of them opened that year.

The product is not the surprise. Affordable lifestyle goods are everywhere.

What it got right was the story, and it licensed that rather than writing it. More than 180 IPs, including Disney, Sanrio and Chiikawa. An American shopper does not need to know the name above the door to recognise what is inside.

Then it turned visits into a relationship. US membership grew more than 150% in 2025, and member sales passed half of total sales for the first time.

Most Asian brands run this in the opposite order. They spend year one explaining who they are to a buyer who never asked. MINISO borrowed recognition first and built its own name on top. By mid-2026 it had 536 stores in North America, against 394 a year earlier.

The hard part of a move into the West is rarely the product. It is the buyer, the price and the story. Rebuilding those three is what our GTM advisory work is for.

No relationship with MINISO. Commentary only.
