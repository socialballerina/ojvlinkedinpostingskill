## Copy

Unitree closed its first day on the Shanghai exchange up 542%.

That is not a robotics story. It is a supply chain story wearing a robot costume.

Read from a desk in London or New York, the takeaway is either "Chinese robotics is a bubble" or "we should have bought the stock". Both miss it.

The reason a humanoid company can clear a Shanghai listing review in 73 days, and price at a 61 billion yuan valuation, is that almost everything it needs is already sitting within an hour's drive of its assembly line.

On the ground in Shenzhen this is unremarkable. Actuators on one floor. Reducers two buildings over. A machinist who will turn a revised part around before the end of the week, then argue with you about the tolerance, because they have built the same part for four other robotics firms this month.

That is the bit you cannot read in a prospectus. The company is not the moat. The one-hour radius is the moat.

We are taking a small group of operators through that radius next month.
