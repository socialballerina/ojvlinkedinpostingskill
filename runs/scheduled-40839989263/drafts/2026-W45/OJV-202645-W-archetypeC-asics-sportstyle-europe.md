## Copy

Asics' lifestyle sneakers grew 77% in Europe in the first half of 2026. Its running shoes grew 10%.

Same company. A lot of the same shoes. A different buyer.

The product was never the problem. Performance running grew in every region Asics sells in.

What changed was who it is for. A runner buys on cushioning and mileage. The SportStyle buyer buys on the archive, the colourway, and who else is wearing it.

So Asics rebuilt the other two things.

The price. Its own filing says the range is "shifting toward a more premium direction, with a focus on products priced above USD100."

The story. Models from the '90s and '00s, restructured as archive lines, and collaborations with designers such as Kiko Kostadinov.

Across Europe, sales were up 29.7% on a currency-neutral basis, with SportStyle leading.

This is the pattern in most Asia-to-West moves we see. The product rarely needs to change. The buyer is a different person, the price has to be rebuilt for how they buy, and the story has to be written for them.

Rebuilding those three is what our GTM advisory work is for.

## Verified facts used

All from the ASICS H1 2026 consolidated financial summary (primary, company filing):

- "Europe showed significant sales growth by +29.7% YoY driven by SPS (+77.0%) and OT (+46.6%)
  with steady growth momentum in P.RUN (+10.1%)." with the footnote "All percentage changes in
  the regional consolidated operating results commentary are shown on a currency-neutral basis".
  Copy uses 77% (SportStyle), 10% (Performance Running) and 29.7%. The hook does not say
  "currency-neutral" for space, but the body does, and both hook figures come from the same
  currency-neutral sentence.
- "Performance Running : All regions showed sales and operating profit growth."
- "The portfolio is shifting toward a more premium direction, with a focus on products priced above
  USD100." Quoted in the copy.
- SportStyle lineup page lists "Restructured Archive", "Archive from '00s", "Archive from '90s",
  and "ASICS X KIKO KOSTADINOV" among the collaborations.

  colourway, and who else is wearing it" are our characterisation, not a sourced claim. Naman to
