Costco stocks almost a tenth of the products a Korean hypermarket does. Not by accident, but by design.

A typical Costco store carries about 4,000 types of item. Korean big-box stores carry close to ten times that.

In its 2024 financial year, Costco Korea's average sales per store were 343.6 billion won. That is four to five times what a Korean big-box store does.

In the year to August 2025 its revenue reached 7.32 trillion won, up 12.1%, from 20 stores. That took it past Homeplus, the No. 2 hypermarket chain, on 6.99 trillion won.

The unlock was pricing architecture, not localisation.

It did not widen the range to match local rivals. It did not lift its margins to local levels either. It targets a 15.1% margin per product, against 26.9% at E-Mart. The membership fee does the rest.

The instinct when entering a new Asian market is to add. Local ranges, local promotions, local everything, until the entrant is a more expensive copy of the incumbent.

Sometimes the model is the product. The job is knowing which parts must change for a market, and which must not.
