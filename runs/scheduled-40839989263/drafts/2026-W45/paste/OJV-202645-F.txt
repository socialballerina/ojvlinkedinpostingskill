Companies say they want a real business in APAC, then hand the whole region to one new hire with a laptop and a global target.

We start with four things:

→ First market
→ Mandate
→ Target
→ Back-up

Then we look at what is underneath them.

Did your hire choose the first market, or inherit a list of six from a slide at head office?

Is their target built from what this market buys, or from your home-market conversion rates?

Can they set price and sign a partner, or does every discount need a late-night call to head office?

When a distributor goes quiet, who do they call? Someone who knows the market, or someone who knows the product?

From the inside this looks like a hiring problem. The numbers are slow, so the answer must be a better country manager. Sometimes it is. More often the job was set up so that nobody could do it, and the next hire inherits the same job.

APAC is not one market. It is 15+ ecosystems with different rules, cultures and buyer behaviour, and nobody recalibrates fifteen of them alone.

A first conversation usually tells us whether we are useful to you. Sometimes the answer is no.
