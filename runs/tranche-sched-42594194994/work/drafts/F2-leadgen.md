## Copy

You can hand a founder's outbound to an agency. You cannot hand over the reason buyers were replying to a founder.

Give us an hour with a founder who still closes every deal and we can usually tell whether that pipeline survives a second seller.

We start with four things:

→ Who replies
→ Why they reply
→ What the founder says on the call
→ What is written down

Then we look at what is underneath them.

Are buyers answering because the message is good, or because the sender is the founder?

Could anyone else on the team explain in two sentences why your last five customers bought?

When a deal stalls, does the founder rescue it with a call nobody else could make?

Is any of that in a playbook, or only in one person's head and inbox?

From the inside, founder-led sales looks like a sales process. Often it is a set of relationships nobody has written down, so the first sales hire looks like the wrong hire.

Across APAC's 15+ ecosystems it gets harder, because what earned the reply in one market rarely earns it in the next.

If you are a founder still closing every deal yourself and need pipeline that does not depend on you, work with us.

DM us, or write to us at hello@oj.ventures
