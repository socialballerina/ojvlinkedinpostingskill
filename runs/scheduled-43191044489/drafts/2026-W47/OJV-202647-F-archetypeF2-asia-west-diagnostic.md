## Copy

Give us an hour with an Asian company moving into the West and we can usually tell which home habit will cost it the first year.

We start with four things:

→ Who signs
→ What they compare you to
→ Price
→ Proof

Then we look at what is underneath them.

Is the person who signs in Germany or Texas the same role that signed at home, or has the budget moved to someone you have never pitched?

When a Western buyer shortlists you, which incumbent are you compared to, and does your deck know its name?

Was your price rebuilt for how that buyer's procurement works, or converted from your home currency?

Is your proof a logo they recognise, or a home-market win they have to take on trust?

From the inside this usually looks like a brand problem. Nobody has heard of us, so the answer must be more marketing. Sometimes it is. More often the product is fine and the buyer, the pricing and the story were never rebuilt for the new room.

APAC is not one market, it is 15+ ecosystems, and the West is not one market either. The job is rapid recalibration, not a rigid playbook.

If you are an Asian company taking a proven product into Western markets and the first meetings are not converting, work with us.

DM us, or write to us at hello@oj.ventures
