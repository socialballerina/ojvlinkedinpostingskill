## Copy

China made 18.9% more industrial robots in September 2026 than a year earlier. It made 17.8% fewer service robots.

Both numbers sit in the same official release, two rows apart.

Read from a desk in London or New York, the China robot story is humanoids doing backflips. That is the part that films well. It is not the part that is compounding.

The National Bureau of Statistics counted 96,752 industrial robots made in September alone, and 794,781 across the first nine months of the year, up 29.1%.

The machines that build things kept growing. The service robots, the category closest to the consumer, went backwards in the month.

On the ground, that demand is not in the showroom. It is in the integrators fitting arms onto lines that already exist, and in factory managers who talk about payback periods rather than demos.

The signal is rarely in the product that trends. It is usually in the dull row of the table that keeps growing.
