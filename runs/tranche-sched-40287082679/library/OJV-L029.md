## Copy

Asian beauty brands call US retail the prize, then start by chasing it. Medicube reached the American shelf last.

APR, the Korean company behind Medicube, built US demand online first, through marketplaces and TikTok Shop, adjusting its products and sales strategy for each channel.

Then the stores. About 1,400 Ulta Beauty stores in 2025. More than 1,500 Target stores in April 2026. Costco shelves in October 2026.

The numbers moved with it. The US was 22% of APR's sales in 2024 and 37% in 2025, a year in which its overseas revenue rose 207% to KRW 1.23 trillion. In the first half of 2026 its North American sales reached 647.9 billion won.

The products were already selling. What changed was which buyer saw the evidence first. A chain's buyer was not asked to take a bet on an unknown Korean brand. The online sales were the pitch.

That is the pattern in most Asia-to-West moves. The hard part is rarely the product. It is the buyer you convince first, the price they compare you against, and the story they need before they put their name on it.
