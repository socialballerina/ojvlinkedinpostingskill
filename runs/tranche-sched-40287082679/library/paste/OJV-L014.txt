You can ship a character across the Pacific in a month. You cannot ship the reason people queue for it.

Pop Mart's own numbers show both halves of that.

In 2025 its revenue from the Americas rose 748.4% to 6.81 billion yuan, 18.3% of total sales. Labubu alone sold 14.16 billion yuan worldwide, up 365.7%.

The product was never the hard part. A collectable is a collectable. What broke through in the West was a story, and it was carried almost entirely by one character.

Then the first half of 2026. Americas revenue fell 16.5% to 1.89 billion yuan, and overseas dropped from 40.3% of the business to 29%.

What Pop Mart did in between is the useful part. It set out to take its US stores from 72 to more than 100 in 2026 to pull sales offline, and products outside Labubu grew to about half of its US revenue.

A viral story wins the first buyer. Owning the shelf and the second character is how you keep them. Neither of those is product work.
