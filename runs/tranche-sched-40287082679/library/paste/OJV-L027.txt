106 investor rejections came before Ukhi's first institutional round. Its co-founder counted every one.

Vishal Vivek wrote the number down himself in April 2026. Ukhi, founded in India in 2019, makes compostable biomaterials. It was a biomaterials company at the first no and at the yes.

What changed was this.

The pitch narrowed. He had been pitching a broad range of hemp applications. In his words, "The turning point came when I stripped the pitch down to one product, one market and one clear path to scale."

The route in changed. His first angel investment, he wrote, "didn't come from a cold email." It came through a recommendation from the IIT Mandi Catalyst incubator.

Then the rounds. In November 2024 Ukhi raised $1.2 million in a pre-seed round led by 100Unicorns. In February 2026 it raised Rs 10.5 crore in a seed round led by Venture Catalysts.

106 nos to a pitch that tried to be everything is not a verdict on the company. It is information about the pitch and the list.
