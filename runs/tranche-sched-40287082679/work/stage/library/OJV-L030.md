## Copy

Give us an hour with a hardware company selling into APAC and we can usually tell which link in the chain is costing it the market.

We start with four things:

→ Certification
→ Channel
→ Landed price
→ Service

Then we look at what is underneath them.

Do you know which certifications each market needs before a distributor will even take the meeting, or are you finding out one rejection at a time?

Who holds your stock? A distributor carrying ten competing brands has no reason to push yours.

Is your price set from your factory cost, or from what the product actually lands at after duty, freight and the channel's margin?

When a unit fails three time zones away, who fixes it, and how many days does the customer wait?

From the inside, a slow hardware market looks like weak demand. Sometimes it is. More often the product never reached the shelf at a price and a service level the buyer could say yes to, and a better product does not fix that.

APAC is not one market. It is 15+ ecosystems with different rules, cultures and buyer behaviour, so the job is rapid recalibration, not a rigid playbook.

If you are selling hardware into APAC and your distributors have gone quiet, work with us.

DM us, or write to us at hello@oj.ventures
