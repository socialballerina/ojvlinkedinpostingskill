American shoppers are supposed to buy clothes on sale. Uniqlo stopped discounting there.

The product was never the problem. Uniqlo spent 17 years in the US before, in 2022, it was poised to book its first annual profit in North America.

What it changed was the price and the story.

The price. "Our competitors offer discounts of 50 per cent or 60 per cent, but we have basically stopped that," its North America chief told Reuters in September 2022. Fast Retailing later described reviewing prices and reducing discounting to lift margins there.

The story. It opened large flagship stores that it calls media beacons, built to raise brand awareness and push e-commerce sales.

In the year to August 2026, North America revenue rose 34.6% to 364.9 billion yen, and business profit rose 53.3% to 67.9 billion yen. For the first time, North America and Europe together outsold Greater China.

The hard part was never the shirts. It was teaching a new buyer to pay full price, and telling them why.
