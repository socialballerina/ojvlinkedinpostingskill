7,500. That is how many restaurants McDonald's had in mainland China by November 2025, eight years after giving up control.

In January 2017 it sold its mainland China and Hong Kong operation to a CITIC-led consortium, with Carlyle, for up to $2.08 billion. CITIC took 52%. McDonald's kept 20%. The stated plan was over 1,500 new restaurants in five years.

By late 2023 the count was 5,600, double the 2017 number. McDonald's then bought out Carlyle and went to 48%. Still a minority. CITIC kept its 52%.

When the numbers came good, McDonald's did not take the majority back. It added stake and left the controlling seat where it was.

We cannot prove the structure caused the growth. We can say McDonald's chose to keep it.
