Cambricon's revenue more than doubled in the third quarter of 2026. Two smaller numbers in the same results say more than that one.

First, the headline. The Beijing chip designer reported CNY3.531 billion of quarterly revenue, up 104.47% year on year, and CNY1.404 billion of net profit, up 147.74%. Both are records since it listed in 2020. Yicai reads it as strong demand in China for homegrown chips.

Now the two smaller numbers.

Operating cash flow for the quarter was negative, at minus CNY83.68 million.

Total liabilities rose 56% from the end of June.

Read from a desk in London, that is one company's good quarter. Together, the three numbers say record profit and negative operating cash in the same three months. That gap is where the real questions sit.

A results table does not tell you who is buying, what they are building on the chips, or how long they will wait for supply. Those answers sit with the people doing the building, and you get them in the room.

The number is the headline. The buyers are the story.
