## Copy

Would you blame demand for a stalled hardware launch in APAC, just because the first market went quiet?

Give us an hour with a hardware company launching in the region and we can usually tell which market will stall it first.

We start with four things:

→ Certification
→ Landed cost
→ Channel
→ After-sales

Then we look at what is underneath them.

Do you know which markets need their own certification before a unit can be sold, and is that time in the launch plan or still in a footnote?

Was your price built from the landed cost in each market, with duties, freight and the distributor's margin, or converted from your home price list?

Is the distributor you signed the right one for the buyer you want, or the one that answered first?

When a unit fails in the field, who swaps it, how fast, and who pays for the courier?

From the inside, a stalled launch looks like a demand problem. Sometimes it is. More often the stock is waiting on paperwork, priced for another market, or sitting with a partner nobody can service.

APAC is not one market. It is 15+ ecosystems with different rules, cultures and buyer behaviour, and for hardware the rules arrive first.

If you are launching hardware into APAC and the first market has stalled, work with us.

DM us, or write to us at hello@oj.ventures
