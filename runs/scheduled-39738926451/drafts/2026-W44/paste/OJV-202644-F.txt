Give us 45 minutes with a startup stuck in a corporate pilot in Asia and we can usually tell whether it will ever become a contract.

We start with four things:

→ Sponsor
→ Budget line
→ Success metric
→ Next signature

Then we look at what is underneath them.

Is your sponsor the person who owns the budget, or the person who liked the demo?

Is the pilot paid for from a line that exists next year, or from an innovation fund that has to be argued for again?

Was the success metric written down before the pilot started, by someone on their side?

Have you met procurement yet, or only the innovation team?

From the inside this looks like patience. The corporate is slow, so the answer must be to wait. Sometimes it is. More often the pilot was set up with nobody on the other side who could say yes, and no amount of waiting fixes that.

APAC is not one market. It is 15+ ecosystems with different rules, cultures and buyer behaviour, and the route to a signature changes with each one.
