You can sign a distributor in a new APAC market. You cannot sign its attention.

That is the gap we look at first when a company tells us its distributor has gone quiet.

We start with four things:

→ Their book
→ Their margin
→ Your enablement
→ Your exit

Then we look at what is underneath them.

How many brands does your distributor already carry in your category, and where do you sit when their sales team plans the week?

Do they make more on your product than on the brand next to it, or are you asking them to work harder for less?

Have you given their reps a reason to bring you up in a meeting, or a price list and a logo pack?

If it is not working in a year, can you leave, or did the contract hand them the market for longer than that?

From the inside this usually looks like a market problem. Sales are slow, so the market must not be ready. Sometimes that is true. More often the market is fine and the product is sitting near the bottom of someone else's list.

APAC is not one market. It is 15+ ecosystems with different rules, cultures and buyer behaviour, and a distributor that works in one rarely carries you into the next.
